Welcome to the Parent Portal

Stay connected with your child's educational journey and engage with teachers effectively. Access real-time updates, communicate directly, and support your child's learning success.

Parent Portal Features

Track Progress

Monitor your child's academic progress in real-time with detailed performance analytics and attendance records. Access grades, assessment results, and teacher feedback to stay informed.

Communication

Easily communicate with teachers through our secure messaging system for updates, concerns, or scheduling parent-teacher meetings. Receive timely notifications about important announcements.

How It Works

Step 1: Create Your Account

Register using your email and create a secure password to access the parent portal. Verify your account through the confirmation link sent to your email.

Step 2: Link to Your Child

Connect your account to your child's profile using the verification code provided by their school. This secure process ensures only authorized parents have access.

Step 3: Access Dashboard

Gain instant access to your personalized dashboard with all the information you need about your child's education. Navigate through intuitive sections for different features.
